# Kady's Glyph Quest — v0.6.0 Release Plan
**Status:** Approved planning scope, not yet implemented.  
**Reference:** Original illustrated match-3 render supplied in the project discussion.  
**Engine:** Godot 4.x / GDScript. **Distribution:** Android standalone APK (never ZIP-only).  
**Platforms:** Phones and tablets from one Android codebase and package.  
**Primary mode:** Finite-board Quest. **Secondary mode:** Arcade.  
**Theme:** A living fantasy puzzle board, real strategic difficulty, and visual world restoration.

## 1. Release goals and priorities
**P0 (release-blocking):** correct dense solvable boards; genuine difficulty validation; functional obstacle interactions; cross-form-factor layout; correct saves; visual clarity; standalone verified APK.  
**P1 (release-defining):** living-gem FX, complete match/gravity sequences, reactive lighting, relic collections, world restoration, ambient world visuals, world music and UI fidelity to reference.  
**P2 (only if performance/stability permits):** device-tilt parallax, extra decorative variations, optional richer particle settings.

**Not in v0.6:** ads, purchases, store submission, paid boosters, 9x9/10x10 device-exclusive boards. Preserve room for future monetization but do not enable it.

## 2. Quest generation, density, and difficulty
- Standard 8x8 board should begin with **48–60 gems**; usually **54–60**.
- Intro boards: 48–54 gems, 0–2 obstacles; standard: 54–60 gems, 1–4 obstacles; advanced: 52–60 gems, 2–6 obstacles; purposeful special layouts may be exceptions.
- No giant empty top half or more than one fully empty starting row in standard levels.
- Limit **permanent** blockers to 0–6 per board, favor small dispersed clusters and interior placement rather than entire blocked columns or two solid side walls.
- Generate distinct board families: Full Board, Center Core, Broken Ruins, Side Chambers, Cross Pattern, Fractured Board. All must remain playable, readable, and intentionally composed.
- No automatic gem refills in Quest. Gravity moves existing gems only; matching removes gems permanently. Complete clear is the victory objective. No legal move with gems still present is a failure.
- Generate candidates using curated board masks and reverse construction, then validate with a **gravity- and obstacle-aware solver**.
- Every released Quest level must have at least one verified booster-free full clear, even with locks, cracked stones, ice, or other obstacles.
- **Do not confuse known solution length with optimal length.** Search for shortcuts and quantify choice complexity, effective branching, forced steps, and dead ends. Reject trivially short alternatives where testing shows them. Where exhaustive optimality proof is too expensive, mark difficulty as estimated and use human playtesting.
- For an 8x8 finite board with at least 3 gems removed per successful matching move, ordinary match-making moves are bounded by the starting gem count / 3. Target compelling **12–18 move** strategies for suitable levels instead of artificial 25+ move requirements.
- No accidental starting matches, invisible deadlocks, unsatisfiable blocker placements, or mandatory paid/free boosters.
- Keep Arcade's replenishing rules as a distinct mode. Quest and Arcade must never share incompatible board serialization.

## 3. Obstacles: actual rules + visual treatments
| Type | Game rule | Animation |
|---|---|---|
| Stone blocker | Fixed; cannot swap or match; interrupts gravity; not required to clear | Local shake/dust on adjacent hit |
| Cracked stone | Adjacent match damages it; final hit frees cell and lets gravity use it | Progressive fractures, debris |
| Locked gem | Gem cannot be swapped until adjacent match unlocks it; then acts as normal gem; must be cleared | Gold chain glow and shatter |
| Frozen gem | Immobile until adjacent thaw; matched frozen gems may clear according to validated puzzle rules | Ice crack/thaw/shatter |
| Rune chain | Two-stage binding released by adjacent matching events | Magical rings lose intensity per hit |
| Ancient relic | Hidden beneath cells or revealed as clearing progresses; collection/progress objective separate from win condition unless level explicitly specifies otherwise | Rising artifact, sparkle, collection animation |

Obstacles must be integrated into solver, hint, Undo, Shuffle, Hammer, save/load, effects and touch selection. Display legible state and accessible indicators. No purely cosmetic obstacle that looks interactive.

## 4. A living animated board (major v0.6 pillar)
### 4.1 Idle gem behavior
- Independent low-amplitude glow/pulse, specular sweeps and glyph lighting; not all tiles in sync.
- Occasional rare sparks; ambient board enchantment should be visible but not visually noisy.
- Keep symbol contrast and legibility stable. Disable most idle motion under **Reduce Motion**.

### 4.2 Match timeline
1. Smooth adjacent swap (~0.15–0.22 sec).
2. Recognized gems illuminate together (~0.08 sec).
3. Charging outline around runes and a rising soft glow (~0.1 sec).
4. White-purple magical core, expanding colored halos, gold spark trails.
5. Fragment/puff disappearance, with 3/4/5+ gem magnitude tiers.
6. Existing gems fall with easing and soft landing bounce.
7. Score and combo multipliers move/fade over the board. Multiple cascades scale up intensity.
8. Controls cannot mutate board state mid-animation; allow queued/blocked input consistently.

**Important:** Deterministic game state updates first, presentation second; interrupted/disabled FX must not affect gameplay, saves or solver outcomes.

### 4.3 Reactive lighting and cleared sockets
- Surrounding gems and stone rim briefly reflect the match color: purple, cyan, green, gold, etc.
- When clearing cells, reveal engraved stone, faint glyphs and segments of a hidden mural or relic beneath the board.
- The board and empty sockets should remain attractive as the finite puzzle drains.

### 4.4 Animated obstacles and victories
- Stones shake; cracked stones crumble; locks break; ice fractures; rune chains fade by stage; relics glow/reveal.
- Ordinary match: small sparkle burst. Four/five+ matches and cascades: increasingly rich effects.
- Perfect clear sequence: last gem explosion, light wave across empty sockets, illustrated relic reveal, sequential star award, victory sting.
- Optional screen shake should be slight, disabled by default or user-togglable, and avoided on Reduce Motion.

## 5. World atmosphere + restoration
- Runes: torch flicker, drifting snow/dust, glowing runestones.
- Egypt: drifting sand, sun shafts, gold artifact highlights.
- East Asia: falling blossoms, lantern glow, soft foliage motion.
- Mixed/Archive: floating glyphs, luminous books, magical particles.
- Layered background art with subtle parallax; optional device-tilt parallax only if user-enabled and stable.
- **World Restoration:** each world begins dim or abandoned. Level and relic milestones gradually illuminate architecture, restore artifacts, and reveal environmental details. Milestones are explicitly recorded in progress saves. Finishing all 12 levels should feel like bringing the world to life.
- **Relic Collection:** optional album available from the main menu with locked/unlocked relic art, world filters, descriptions, and progress counts. Clearing levels unlocks fragments/relics without requiring purchases.

## 6. Render-fidelity UI pass
Compare **actual Godot screenshots** against the reference at every review iteration.
- Illustrated, flowing gold-purple title emblem instead of an oversized flat plaque.
- Compact score/moves, small pause; elegantly integrated parchment level ribbon.
- Slim, bright objective parchment with a clear progress bar; readable on phone.
- Thin blue-stone frame *behind* gems. Visible beveled corners and tile edges.
- Jewel materials with stronger shine, glyph engravings, lighting and readable contrast.
- Bottom action buttons: illustrated icons, visible **Hint / Shuffle / Hammer / Undo** labels, separate counts, touch-friendly states. Avoid cramped lettering.
- All screens share the same art system (menu, worlds, levels, gameplay, pause, victory, failure, settings, relic album).
- No decorative lettering baked into backgrounds underneath the live UI. No clipping/overlap at safe areas.

## 7. Responsive Android phone + tablet layouts (REQUIRED)
**ONE APK.** Do not create independent tablet/phone products or fragment progress.
### Existing behavior to replace
v0.5's 720x1440 design uses a uniform scale `min(screen_width / 720, screen_height / 1440)`, centered with background fill. A broad tablet therefore enlarges the phone layout but may leave substantial unused side space; it is **not yet a fully adaptive tablet UI**.

### Target presentations
| Layout | Board | Auxiliary UI |
|---|---|---|
| Phone portrait | Board occupies nearly usable width, 8x8 square cells | Compact top HUD; boosters along bottom |
| Tablet portrait | Larger board scaled to available safe-area width/height | Richer level/collection details in side/marginal spaces |
| Tablet landscape | Large square board primarily left or center-left, maximize height | Dedicated right-hand panel with objective, score, pause, boosters, relic status |

- Calculate layout from actual **safe-area rectangles, logical display dimensions and available space**, not just raw pixel resolution or assumed model name.
- Use Godot `Control`, anchors, containers and/or a tested layout coordinator instead of scaling all 720x1440 elements as one monolith. Preserve square tile hitboxes.
- Adapt margins, board extent, font sizes and touch targets. The same underlying 8x8 puzzle remains identical on phones and tablets.
- Support portrait phones. Add tablet landscape behavior through orientation handling and responsive recalculation; test how orientation switches interact with Android cutouts, system bars, resume and autosave. Do not stretch artwork non-uniformly.
- Keep high-resolution art for large tablet screens; lower image resolution/effect detail automatically or optionally on slower hardware.
- Avoid redesigning gameplay rules per device. Same game, world music, progress, boosters and level data.
- Test screenshots at **1080x2400 phone**, **1200x1920 tablet portrait**, **1920x1200 tablet landscape**, plus more squarish 3:4 ratios. Also test the actual Pixel 10 and D11 tablet when accessible.
- Rotation/split-screen/resizing must not restart a puzzle, erase progress or misplace tap coordinates. No clipped HUD, stretched gems, tiny touch controls or huge blank margins.

## 8. Audio and controls
- Retain five original themed tracks: menu, Runes, Egypt, East Asia, Mixed. Music transitions must be smooth and each world play its own track.
- Add tasteful world-appropriate ambience and distinct matching, stone, lock, relic, combo, fail and victory stings.
- Separate music/SFX volumes, mute behavior, optional background/lighting/effects intensity, screen shake toggle and accessibility settings.
- Visual, audio and vibration feedback should be event driven and not frame-rate dependent.
- Focus performance on fluid **60 FPS where achievable**; profile on actual mobile and tablet hardware. Use pooled particles, shader budgets, limited simultaneity, and lower-effects profile.

## 9. Saving, progression and accessibility
- Save board, lock/ice/chain states, collectibles, restoration milestones, scores, Undo snapshots, unlocked levels, settings, world and music preferences.
- Migrate older completed level/star history; do not load obsolete in-progress puzzles as new layouts.
- Preserve the standard application ID; maintain a separately packaged preview for side-by-side testing if debug certificates differ.
- Reduce Motion, High Contrast, Larger Text, togglable effects intensity, accessible glyph identifiers and responsive touch targets.
- Never tie necessary level completion to advertising or purchasing.

## 10. Implementation units and verification
1. **Solver and density:** dense board fixtures, difficulty/shortcut checker, coverage metrics and all-level certifications.
2. **Obstacle engine:** state transitions and cross-feature tests (gravity/hints/shuffle/hammer/undo/save).
3. **Animation layer:** event timeline, gem shaders, particle pools, reactive rim lights, backdrop murals and obstacle/Victory FX.
4. **Art & UI:** reference-matched assets, consistent screens, adaptive phone/tablet layout engine.
5. **Collection/restoration:** collectibles metadata, artwork, milestone-driven scene changes, UI and saves.
6. **Audio & optimization:** ambience/cues, transition tests, effects profiles.
7. **Release QA:** all 48 levels pass automated solutions and solver checks, UI screenshots at all target ratios, Android emulator installation, actual Pixel 10 and D11 testing where available, APK signatures, directly published standalone debug APK.

### Definition of Done
- Boards start attractively dense and are genuinely more challenging than v0.5, **not merely validated by one long solution**.
- All 48 Quest boards remain booster-free completable and never require refill.
- New obstacles work correctly under gravity and saves; collectible/world restoration progress persists.
- Animated board looks alive at idle and has visibly stronger match/cascade/victory reactions without confusing gameplay.
- Gameplay reference composition is recognizably close, not just similarly colored.
- A single app genuinely adapts to phone portrait, tablet portrait and tablet landscape.
- Music and effects operate correctly; performance and accessibility controls are functional.
- Publish **standalone APK only, not a ZIP**. Android device tests and user feedback required before production-readiness claims.
