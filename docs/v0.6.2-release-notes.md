# Runefall v0.6.2: Fair Quest Update

**Distribution:** Direct standalone Android APKs only. Same package IDs as v0.6.1 and higher Android version code (9). Both release APKs will be signed using the same persistent GammaPi testing key as v0.6.1.

## What's changed
- Quest **1 star at 75%**, **2 stars at 85%**, **3 stars at 100%** of the starting gems cleared. Percentages are checked using exact integer comparisons; displayed percentages cannot round a losing board up to a pass.
- **Claim Victory** appears when the player qualifies; they can finish immediately or continue matching for a higher rating.
- If the board runs out of legal matches **after qualifying**, it is a victory instead of a failure.
- If the board runs out of moves **before 75%**, the player receives **one free rescue opportunity per attempt**, with no ad or payment. Rescue reshuffles existing unlocked gems into a board with a legal move, when possible. If no safe reshuffle exists, it removes up to three existing gems without spawning replacements; the level may still fail.
- Hint recommendations prioritize the recorded, validated level solution whenever the player remains on its path. Off-path hints consider subsequent moves and deadlock risk instead of merely selecting the largest immediate match. Off-path hints are heuristic, not a solvability guarantee.
- New completion and recovery screens and touch controls for phone portrait, tablet portrait and tablet landscape.
- Current progress, relics, stars, settings, world-specific music and the original Arcade mode are preserved. No ads or payments are enabled in this release.
