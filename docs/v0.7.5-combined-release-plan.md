# Runefall v0.7.5 — Unified Navigation, Rewards & Gameplay

**Status: Approved scope, planning only. Not yet implemented.**
**Replaces:** separate v0.7.2 update. All v0.7.2 changes ship with v0.7.5.

## P0 — Home, reward feedback and responsive design

### Relic celebrations and restoration
- Milestone relics at levels 3, 6, 9 and 12 in every world.
- On FIRST unlock only, detect newly acquired relic by comparing saved reward states. After the level results, open a skippable full-screen RELIC FOUND reveal with named art, gold burst, runic particles, sound sting and world restoration change.
- Buttons: Continue and View Collection. World-restoration bar must advance immediately, including new scenery.
- Record pending reward events, newly seen versus unseen relics and achievement acknowledgements in saved data, surviving restart/background/resume. No repeated unlock animations for replayed levels.
- Already owned relics after upgrading receive an unobtrusive NEW indicator in the collection, not a replay of every old victory.
- Honor Reduce Motion and volume settings, including a clean static version of the unlock card.

### Clean Home screen
- Keep Runefall logo and tagline, compact Current Adventure (world, level, Quest/Arcade), ONE prominent Begin/Continue action, and four illustrated world cards.
- Four world cards are the ONLY world-selection control. REMOVE redundant left/right carousel arrows.
- Replace giant Settings button with a recognizable top-right gear icon and a large touch target.
- Replace giant bottom Progress and Relic Collection buttons with compact clearly labeled actions/badges.
- Move Choose a Level into a small action on Current Adventure that opens the new level map, eliminating the duplicate full-width button.
- Correct the hardcoded v0.6.1 footer; use the current version dynamically from BrandConfig.
- Preserve Android Back navigation and a short path from app start to gameplay.

### Tablet and background repairs
- Fix visible background seams/repeated bands, unintended cropping, oversized scaling and clipping; keep essential artwork in a safe center area.
- Use layered background artwork and genuinely higher-resolution source assets for low-resolution backgrounds, borders and buttons; do not simply upscale low-resolution PNGs.
- Preserve the MSDF typography improvements from v0.7.1. Ensure proper responsive layout and hit boxes at 720x1440, 864x1536, 1200x1920 and 1920x1200 plus Android system insets.

## P1 — Original v0.7.5 expansion

### Special gemstone combinations
- Match 4: creates row/column-clearing empowered rune.
- Match 5: creates a wildcard matching a chosen symbol/type.
- T/L/cross match: creates a local magical blast.
- Add distinctive textures, animations, sound cues, tutorials and reduced-motion alternatives.
- Preserve Quest finite-board no-refill behavior. Specials use an existing cell, never spawn replacement stones.
- Integrate effects with matching, lock/ice/stone interactions, deterministic solver, Undo, Shuffle, Hint, saved game state and automatic victory. Do not invalidate the existing 48 certified solutions.
- Arcade can retain different refilling logic.

### World map and level progression
- Four themed maps, each with 12 selectable level nodes, lock/current/won states, 1–3 star ratings and milestone relic nodes at 3/6/9/12.
- Show next relic, world-restoration stage and available quests. Navigate here from Current Adventure or after winning.

### Achievements and player statistics
- Offline awards for first match/win, perfect clear, combos, relics, fully restored worlds and exploration.
- Persist unique IDs and one-time unlock banners without duplicates, including restarts and interrupted reward animations.
- Progress shows total stars, completion %, best scores, relics, achievements and completion per world.

## Explicit exclusions

- No enabled ads, purchase SDKs, subscriptions, shop checkout or account requirement until v0.8.0.
- No new public name/package ID, forced save reset, separate tablet APK or changes to Quest's 75/85/100 star thresholds/free recovery.

## Release gates / acceptance tests

1. Fresh completion of Runes level 3: one Frost Rune unlock animation, restoration becomes 1/4 and collection updates. Replaying level 3 cannot award twice.
2. Previously earned relics display as owned after update with appropriate once-only NEW indicators. Closing the app mid-celebration never loses an unlock.
3. Home has exactly one prominent Play control, four world cards, one Settings gear, and compact Progress/Relics controls, without carousel arrows or redundant giant navigation.
4. Background artwork displays without seams, excessive cropping or blurry text across all required portrait/landscape phone/tablet sizes.
5. Special powers have defined creation/activation rules and safely integrate with Undo, save/load, obstacles, no-refill gameplay and certified solvability checks.
6. Map and achievements remain correct offline, including resumed play and upgrade from v0.7.1.
7. Complete all existing gameplay, star-threshold, rescue and accessibility tests. Capture actual Godot screenshots for Home, maps, relic reveal and tablet layouts.
8. Publish only two independently signed, installable standalone APKs (Preview/Debug), preserving the persistent signing certificate and application IDs, with Android 16 installation/upgrade verification and checksums. Physical phone/tablet verification remains a separate release gate.

## One combined release

Development order: navigation/background and save-event migration; relic reveal and restoration; special gems and solver tests; maps and achievements; QA/testing; v0.7.5 APK publication.

Then v0.8.0 monetization, v0.9.0 Play testing/compliance and v1.0.0 launch.
